Long-Term Memory — journey wireframes

Greybox layouts for six user journeys. Structure and information hierarchy only: no visual design, no final copy. Every frame carries a feasibility mark, and every figure in them belongs to one worked example: a vault of 118 memories, and one extraction run of 1,142 candidates. Amber notes tie a frame to the findings it answers.
