Introduction

I moved a three-week story off Marinara Engine's built-in chat summaries and into the Long-Term Memory vault, then wrote down everything the system did along the way. This document collects 83 findings with source code references.

This is a lot of shit. I tried editing down the Claude slop but it's still a slopfest. Feel free to take as much or as little from this as you like.

Scope

The review covers the package's review, apply, diagnostic and error surfaces, its extraction and projection pipeline, and its retrieval and injection path as far as the prompt.

Out of scope:

  • Lorebook interop
  • World-state agent or any other agent interactions
  • Reconciliation between roleplay time and wall-clock time
  • Multi-persona setups
  • Group chats
  • Retrieval quality

How to read a finding

Every finding carries four fields. Severity, evidence grade and effort are independent of one another, so a critical finding can be a small fix and a low-severity one can be a large one.

FieldValuesWhat each value means
Severitycritical · high · medium · low Critical marks a really bad bug or a failure of fundamental functionality. High marks an interface that lies to the user, or a constraint most people hit. Medium marks something workaroundable through the API, misleading rather than incorrect, or a constraint only a power user reaches. Low marks something better prompting fixes, something that may be model-dependent, or a clunky interface a user surmounts by poking around.
Evidence gradesource · observed · measured · reported Source means Claude checked the source code. Observed means it happened during the migration and left an artifact. Measured means a number came out of analysis from user activity. Reported means I personally ran into and discussed the issue with Claude.
Effortsmall · medium · large Claude estimate of effort to address a given issue.
Bug tagpresent or absent Distinguish between bugs and UI/UX issues, missing features, etc.
